# My Favorite Series: House, M.D.

![House M.D. Logo](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/house-tv-show-pictures-and-images--898608931880860202/)

**Genre:** Medical Drama, Mystery, Comedy  
**Creator:** David Shore  
**Main Character:** Dr. Gregory House (played by *Hugh Laurie*)

## 🧠 What I Like About It
- The medical cases are complex and fascinating  
- Dr. House’s sarcastic and genius personality  
- Each episode mixes logic, humor, and human emotion  
- The show teaches critical thinking and empathy in unexpected ways  

> “Everybody lies.” — **Dr. Gregory House**

### 💊 Fun Facts
1. The show ran for **8 seasons** from *2004 to 2012*.  
2. Hugh Laurie used an American accent so well that producers didn’t know he was British at first!  
3. Each episode follows a mystery format similar to detective shows.

#### 💬 My Thoughts
*House, M.D.* shows that intelligence without compassion can be both a gift and a curse.  
It reminds me that solving problems requires both logic **and** understanding people.
